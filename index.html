      My method

      A weekly loop, not a list of tips

      1. 01

        Time-block

        Every goal gets a slot on the calendar, not leftover time.

      2. 02

        Break it down

        Big assignments become small tasks. The first one takes 20 minutes.

      3. 03

        Track it

        Calendar check-offs, a practice-hours log, an assignment log.

      4. 04

        Review & adjust

        Saturday: compare plan vs. reality, fix next week.

      Why it fits me: it's how we already work at Nx iD. Plan a sprint, split work into tickets, track it, run a retro.
